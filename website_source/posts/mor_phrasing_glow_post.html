Definition: A near-black reading surface lit from within by soft lavender. Panels, headings, and links carry a faint periwinkle glow — candlelit but electric — over an antique letterpress serif.

Palette:

  • Base: #080808 · Panel: #080808 → #121212 (135°) · Elevated: #141414
  • Text: #ECECEB · Muted: #9d9db8
  • Accent: #b9b9fa · Border: #2b2b2b

Typeface: IM Fell English (Georgia / serif fallback) — 17px base, 1.3 scale, 1.6 line height, headings at normal weight.

Signature Effects:

  • Two fixed radial lavender glows across the page background.
  • A rune-etched frame on every post panel (.mor-post / .runelite-panel), RuneLite style.
  • Lavender box-shadow bloom on post hover; soft heading/link glow that brightens on hover.
  • Lavender text-selection highlight; glowing header underline.

Buttons: 6px radius, 1px border, no uppercasing.
